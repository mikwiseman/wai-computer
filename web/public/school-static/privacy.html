School website privacy

Updated 9 September 2026

This notice covers the public education pages on wai.computer and enquiries sent to the school.

Who handles your information

WaiWai, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, operates these school pages. Our company address is 1111B S Governors Ave, STE 28908, Dover, DE 19904, USA. Contact hi@wai.computer about this notice or your information.

Browsing these pages

You can read the school pages without creating an account. Our hosting systems process technical request information, such as IP address, requested page, browser information and request time, to deliver the website and keep it secure. The school pages do not contain advertising trackers or an online enrolment form.

Contacting the school

When you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to share. We use this to respond, discuss course options and arrange the next steps you request. Our email and hosting providers process information as needed to deliver those services.

Enquiries should come from parents or guardians. For an initial conversation, a child’s age and interests are enough. Please do not send a child’s full name, school, address, medical information, photographs or recordings with an enquiry.

Enrolment and children’s information

This public website does not create student accounts or collect lesson recordings. The school explains the information needed for a course and obtains the required parent or guardian permissions as part of enrolment, before the relevant learning activities begin.

Linked projects and services

Student demos on this website may save game progress and preferences in your browser. Some demos let visitors submit an in-game nickname, a score, a comment, a quiz result or a multiplayer room action. These submissions use the school’s project hosting service and may be visible to other visitors. Use a made-up nickname and do not enter personal details. You can clear locally saved progress through your browser settings. Contact us to request removal of a submitted record, identifying the project and nickname. Some projects load fonts or other assets from external providers, which receive the technical request information needed to serve them. Links to other websites are governed by those websites’ privacy notices.

Retention and your choices

Enquiry correspondence is retained while needed to respond and manage the requested relationship, and where necessary for legal obligations or resolving disputes. Write to us to request access, correction or deletion of the information you have provided.

The WaiComputer recording application has a separate application privacy policy.
