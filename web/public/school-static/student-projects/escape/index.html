About the game

A small browser game. A strange world, a paddle and a ball. Beat the system and escape. Perfect for a quiet break.

Who it is for

Anyone who wants a quiet ten-minute game on a break or while traveling.

How to play

  • Slide your finger up and down to move the paddle
  • Return the ball to the system
  • Score 5 points to get out
