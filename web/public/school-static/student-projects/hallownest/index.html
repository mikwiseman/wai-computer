About the game

For those who finished Hollow Knight and want more. Not a remake or a mod — a new world, a new story, that same feeling.

An empty world after a catastrophe. A wanderer stands alone among the ruins, with history hidden in fragments and survivors' voices. No long explanations: characters speak in riddles and hints. Find the answers yourself.

The Watcher's final entry

It had not vanished. It had gone nowhere — only fallen quiet, like fire beneath the ashes. The seal kept it locked away, and we called that victory. But victory means the enemy is dead. It was alive. It was waiting. And when the seal began to crack from within, the people here had already forgotten how to be afraid. That was when the wanderer came. That was when everything began again.
