🎮 How to play

💰 1

Buy insects with gold. Each fighter has a different price. Choose carefully!

⚔️ 2

Press BATTLE! The computer builds its own army and the fight begins automatically!

🏆 3

Win to earn more gold for an even bigger army in the next round!

✨ Game secrets

🪲 The rhinoceros beetle's horn knocks enemies back!
🕷️ A spider's web slows enemies to a crawl!
🐝 A swarm of five wasps can sting even the biggest beetle into submission!
🤖 The computer has different personalities. Sometimes it picks tanks, sometimes a wasp swarm!
🦋 The butterfly does not fight. It heals your army with magical pollen!
🦟 The mosquito is a vampire. Each bite heals itself!
🦂 After a scorpion stings, its target glows green and loses health to poison!
🐜 An ant costs just 1 gold. Ten coins can buy ten ants!
🪰 Flies are hard to hit. A third of attacks miss!
🪳 A cockroach is hard to kill. Once per battle, it survives a fatal blow!
🐌 The snail has the most health in the game: 260! A real living wall.
🐞 The ladybug's shell reflects almost half the damage!
🌿 The arena has bushes where insects hide, making them harder to hit!
🕳️ Watch out for pits! Fall in and you cannot fight. Flying insects stay safe!
⭐ Level up: each victory makes your fighters stronger, up to level 5!
🪨 A large rock blocks the way. Hide behind it to avoid enemies.
👑 THREE bosses: Giant Hornet 🐝, Spider Queen 🕷️ and Ant Queen 🐜! A new boss every fifth round, each with a special attack!
🗺️ Four arenas: Meadow 🌿, Swamp 🐸 with sticky puddles, Desert 🌵 and Night 🌙, where hiding is easier.
✨ A level 5 insect becomes GOLDEN — glowing gold and gaining a superpower!
🎒 Items: Acorn Helmet 🌰, Leaf Shield 🍃 and Honey Drop 🍯. Buy them before battle!
🧠 The AI levels up each round too. Victory will not be easy!
💰 You have 1,000 coins for the whole game! Spend wisely. Victory earns coins, while a defeated army returns home with your money.
🎉 The game has 100 CHARACTERS! Regular bugs, elite fighters like Ninja Fly 🥷 and Emperor Scorpion 👑, plus six elements: Forest 🌲, Steel 🛡️, Shadow 🌑, Electric ⚡, Lava 🌋 and Cosmic 🚀 versions of each insect!
👬 Two-player mode! You and a friend secretly build teams in turn, then watch to see whose army is stronger!
✨ Each element has a superpower. Forest 🌲 fighters heal themselves, Steel 🛡️ reflects damage, Shadow 🌑 teleports behind enemies and Electric ⚡ stuns!
📖 Bestiary — collect them all: see which fighters you have tried and how often they have won. Unlock all 100!
💀 Hardcore mode for the brave: lose and your coins are gone! Rewards are doubled.
